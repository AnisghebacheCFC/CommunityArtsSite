About Community Arts

Connecting generations through creativity.

Community Arts is an independent, community-led initiative rooted in Elephant & Castle, Southwark.

We bring younger and older generations together through accessible, hands-on arts workshops, creating opportunities for connection, creative expression and shared learning.

Working with students, emerging artists and local residents, we aim to reduce social isolation, strengthen community relationships and provide aspiring creatives with practical experience and paid opportunities where funding allows.

Our workshops explore botanical printing, natural dyes, collage, calligraphy, textiles and knitting, encouraging participants to experiment, exchange skills and discover new creative interests.

Beginning with workshops at Draper Hall, our ambition is to build a wider network of creative activities throughout Southwark, connecting local community spaces, residents and artists.
