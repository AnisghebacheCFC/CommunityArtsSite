A photograph on my wall

While researching workshops, I came across photographs from a collaboration between the Singapore Red Cross and Schneider Electric. One in particular stayed with me: two participants from different generations and backgrounds making small figures together. I printed it on my Polaroid printer and put it on my wall.

In my own experience working with older people, I’ve sometimes encountered uncertainty about younger people’s intentions. That made me interested in how a shared activity might allow familiarity and trust to develop through doing something together.

What struck me in this photograph was the sense of collaboration and a brief moment of joy. It gave me a reference for the kind of interaction I hoped Community Arts could make possible.
