A different way of looking

For this session, we brought in a Guzmania ‘Hope’. Its vivid red centre, with shades of pink and white towards the top, made it an interesting subject for drawing. What appears to be its flower is largely a display of coloured bracts, modified leaves surrounding the smaller flowers.

I wanted to keep the plant intact, so rather than use it for printing, we invited people to draw it or simply enjoy having it on the table. We also brought some cut flowers, with yellow and blue colouring, which cost around £3. These gave us another subject to work from without spending much.

Dave drew both the flowers and the Guzmania. The speed with which he produces recognisable forms always interests me. Within seconds, he has a drawing on the page, and his colouring is equally assured. There’s a directness to his approach that I enjoy seeing. He and Liza also have an easy rapport, with support offered naturally and room for him to get on with his work.

Other participants made occasional prints. Having different materials available meant that people could choose what interested them, rather than everyone following the same activity.

I’d like to retain that flexibility as the workshops develop. This time, the plant’s appearance suggested drawing, and preserving it felt more worthwhile than trying to make it serve our usual printing process. Each session gives us an opportunity to reconsider what we offer and how people might want to take part.
